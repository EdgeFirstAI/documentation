# COCO Dataset

The **COCO (Common Objects in Context)** dataset is a **large-scale, richly annotated image dataset** designed to advance the development of models for **object detection, segmentation, keypoint detection, and image captioning** in computer vision. COCO was created to enable models to learn **object recognition in realistic settings** where objects:

- Appear in **complex scenes** with clutter and occlusion.
- Vary in scale, pose, and lighting.
- Are seen in **context**, not isolated from their environment.

It emphasizes **contextual relationships** among objects, enabling the study of scene understanding beyond simple object detection.
