# Subscription

The Subscription page allows administrators to view and manage the organization's subscription
plan and saved payment methods.  To access this page, navigate to **Admin Console** from the
user menu, then click **Subscription** from the side navigation.

{{ figure("../assets/user/subscription-plans.png", "Subscription Management") }}

## Current Plan

The **Current Plan** section shows the active subscription tier and any **Scheduled Change**
that will take effect at the next billing cycle.  The billing currency is displayed below
the plan selector.

## Card List

The **Card List** section shows all credit cards saved to the organization.  Click
**Add Credit Card** to register a new card.  On each saved card row you can:

- Click **Default** to make that card the primary payment method.
- Click the delete icon to remove the card from the account.

## Billing Period

Use the **Monthly** / **Save by choosing annual plan** toggle to switch between monthly
and annual billing.  Annual billing reduces the effective monthly cost.

| Pay as you go | Yes | Yes | Yes | Custom | Not listed |

To change your plan, click the **Choose** button on the desired plan card.  The change takes
effect at the start of the next billing cycle unless a downgrade is applied immediately.

!!! tip "Annual Savings"
    Switching to the annual billing period reduces the effective monthly cost compared to
    paying month-to-month.
